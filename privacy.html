Privacy Policy

Laddoo Waddoo, by Yansh Labs · Last updated 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Laddoo Waddoo game ("the app") uses, and why. The app is made by Yansh Labs ("we"). If you have any question, write to info@yanshlabs.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The app has no sign-in and no account.

To stop cheating on the daily limit of reward videos, the app gets a random ID when it first goes online (from Google Firebase, our server provider). It says nothing about who you are. Each reward video goes out with that ID and the date, and when you finish one, Google AdMob tells our server, which counts it for that day. That count is all we keep, and it is deleted after about a week. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, ends the link between your phone and the ID.

Your game progress (levels finished, stars, best scores, coins, lives, boosters) and your sound settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. If Android's own backup is on for your phone, Android may keep a copy in your Google account and bring it back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Otherwise, uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Ads

The app shows rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example, to get an extra life or spin). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • information about the device and app, such as the model, operating system and how ads perform
  • diagnostic and crash information from the ads software

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We keep none of this information. Google keeps it only as long as its own policies allow; you can see and delete the data Google holds about you at myactivity.google.com, and resetting your advertising ID (see below) cuts its link to your phone.

The ads are kept family-friendly: no ad rated above PG (parental guidance), and ads about dating, gambling, alcohol, sex, weight loss, cosmetic procedures, drugs and supplements, get-rich-quick schemes, politics, religion and similar topics are blocked.

Phone permissions

  • Internet, to load the ads, and to check today's date and time with our website (yanshlabs.com), so lives and daily rewards follow the real clock (that check sends nothing about you or your game), and to count finished reward videos with the random ID described above.
  • Notifications, only if you turn on "remind me" in the lives panel. The reminder that your lives are full is set up on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or exact location.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time with "Privacy choices" in the app's settings (the gear on the level map).
  • On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on the phone).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play. Every reward can also be earned by playing or waiting.

Children

The app is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information sent by the ads software and to our server is encrypted in transit. Only you can read your own video count. Game progress stays on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
